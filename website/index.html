FAQ

Questions

Is Why Not You available yet?

Not on the store yet. It’s launching on Google Play soon. Until then you can join the beta: email support@whynotyou.app with the subject “Beta tester” and we’ll add your Google account to the test.

Why do you need beta testers?

Google Play asks new developer accounts to run a closed test with real testers before an app can be published to everyone. Testers get the app early, for free, and help us find problems before launch.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Alarms, your plan and the voice that reads it all run on your phone, so the wake-up works without a connection. Connecting an AI assistant needs the internet.

Which AI assistants can I connect?

Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Grok and Le Chat, through an MCP connector. Gemini and Copilot aren’t supported yet. Connecting an assistant is part of Pro.

What is an MCP connector?

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is an open standard that lets AI assistants use outside tools. You add the Why Not You connector in your assistant’s settings and sign in. After that the assistant can read and change your alarms, plans and app limits when you ask it to.

What’s free and what’s Pro?

Free: alarms, the spoken wake-up plan, typing your plan yourself, and Phone down for 2 apps. Pro adds calendar events read to you, your AI planning your day, unlimited app limits with strict mode, and weekly screen time stats. Compare plans.

How do the trial and cancelling work?

Pro subscriptions start with a 7-day free trial. Billing goes through Google Play, so you cancel in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions. Cancel before the trial ends and you won’t be charged.

Do you sell my data or show ads?

No and no. Your alarms, plans and voice stay on your phone. If you connect an AI assistant, we store only what’s needed for it to work. Read the privacy policy.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not right now. Why Not You is Android only.
