Why Not You vs Opal

Different apps for different problems. Here’s an honest look at who each one is for, so you can pick the right one — even if it isn’t ours.

Who each app is for

Why Not You

An alarm that hands you your plan

Stop the alarm and your plan for the day is read aloud in a natural on-device voice. You write it the evening before, or your AI assistant does. During the day, Phone down reminds you of it when you’ve spent too long in an app.

Best for: People who want to start the day with a plan and be reminded of it when they drift into apps — with an alarm, a spoken plan and their own AI assistant in one place.

Opal

What it focuses on

Opal focuses on screen time and focus: blocking distracting apps during sessions you schedule and giving you insight into your phone use. Check their site for current Android availability and features.

Best for: People whose main goal is focused work time and detailed screen-time control.

Which should you choose?

The short answer

If deep focus sessions and screen-time control are the whole point, a dedicated focus app like Opal is worth a look. If you want your phone limits tied to a morning routine and a plan you actually hear, Why Not You is built for that.
