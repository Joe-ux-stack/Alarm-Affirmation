Why Not You vs Sleep as Android

Different apps for different problems. Here’s an honest look at who each one is for, so you can pick the right one — even if it isn’t ours.

Who each app is for

Why Not You

An alarm that hands you your plan

Stop the alarm and your plan for the day is read aloud in a natural on-device voice. You write it the evening before, or your AI assistant does. During the day, Phone down reminds you of it when you’ve spent too long in an app.

Best for: People who care less about sleep data and more about starting the day with a plan — spoken aloud, written the night before or by their AI.

Sleep as Android

What it focuses on

Sleep as Android is a long-running Android app focused on sleep tracking and smart wake-up timing based on your sleep, with a deep set of options.

Best for: People who want to track and analyse their sleep and like configuring their alarm in detail.

Which should you choose?

The short answer

If you want sleep data and fine-grained alarm settings, Sleep as Android is the more specialised tool. If you want a simple alarm that hands you your plan when you wake and helps you keep the phone down afterwards, try Why Not You.
