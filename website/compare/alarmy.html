Why Not You vs Alarmy

Different apps for different problems. Here’s an honest look at who each one is for, so you can pick the right one — even if it isn’t ours.

Who each app is for

Why Not You

An alarm that hands you your plan

Stop the alarm and your plan for the day is read aloud in a natural on-device voice. You write it the evening before, or your AI assistant does. Each alarm can also carry its own message, and your AI can set up a whole week of them.

Best for: People who wake up fine but want a clear first thought for the day: their plan, spoken aloud, and a nudge to stay off the phone later.

Alarmy

What it focuses on

Alarmy is widely known for wake-up “missions” — tasks you complete before the alarm will stop, designed to make sure you actually get out of bed.

Best for: People who sleep through ordinary alarms or turn them off half-asleep, and want an alarm that’s hard to dismiss.

Which should you choose?

The short answer

If your problem is getting up, an alarm built around missions like Alarmy may suit you better. If your problem is what happens after — starting the day without a plan and losing the morning to your phone — that’s what Why Not You is built for.
