Why Not You vs one sec

Different apps for different problems. Here’s an honest look at who each one is for, so you can pick the right one — even if it isn’t ours.

Who each app is for

Why Not You

An alarm that hands you your plan

Stop the alarm and your plan for the day is read aloud in a natural on-device voice. You write it the evening before, or your AI assistant does. During the day, Phone down reminds you of it when you’ve spent too long in an app.

Best for: People who want their screen-time reminder connected to their day: the same plan you hear when you wake up is what Phone down reminds you of.

one sec

What it focuses on

one sec focuses on adding a short, deliberate pause before you open distracting apps, so you notice the habit and can choose not to continue.

Best for: People who mainly want to cut down on reflexive app opening, and don’t need an alarm or daily planning.

Which should you choose?

The short answer

If all you want is a speed bump before opening an app, a dedicated tool like one sec may be all you need. If you want the whole day to hang together — plan at night, hear it in the morning, get reminded of it when you drift — Why Not You does that in one app.
